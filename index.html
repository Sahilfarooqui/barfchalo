      🛡️ Drive safe in snow

      ⛓️

      Carry chains

      Most 2WD cars need snow chains after fresh snow. Police often allow only 4×4 or chains.

      🌙

      Check the night before

      Call your stay or check local traffic police pages. Roads close fast after heavy snow.

      ⛽

      Fill up in the last big town

      Pumps are rare high up. Keep the tank above half. CNG and EV charging are scarce in the hills.

      🧊

      Watch for black ice

      Shaded bends freeze in the morning. Drive slow, low gear, no sudden braking.

      🌅

      Drive in daylight

      Reach your stay before dark. Mountain roads at night are the biggest risk.

      🎫

      Permits

      North Sikkim, Tsomgo, Zuluk and some border areas need permits. Self-drive may not be allowed.
