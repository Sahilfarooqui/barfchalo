🔒 Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

BarfChalo is a free trip planner. We collect as little as possible.

No accounts

There are no sign-ups, logins or passwords. We don't ask for your name, phone number or email.

Your location stays in your browser

If you tap “My location”, your browser asks your permission and gives the position to the page only to pick your starting point. It is not stored or sent to BarfChalo. To work out routes, weather and place names, the page sends the start and destination coordinates directly from your browser to free map services: OpenStreetMap Nominatim and Photon (place names), the OSRM router (routes) and Open-Meteo (weather and elevation). Each of these has its own privacy policy.

Anonymous visit counts

We count page views, unique visitors, trips opened and share-button taps using the free Abacus counter service. Only a number goes up. No cookies are set and no name, IP-based profile or location is stored by us. To avoid counting the same browser twice, a small “already counted” flag is kept in your browser's local storage. You can clear it at any time. The totals are public on our reach page.

Share links

A trip link you share contains the start point, car, group size and destination you chose, so that the person who opens it sees the same trip. Only share it with people you are happy to show that to.

Third-party content

Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap, photos from Wikimedia Commons and fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser loads these directly from those services. Links to Booking.com, Airbnb and Google Maps take you to their sites, where their policies apply.
